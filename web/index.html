OCR reads each detected dimension and proposes its line and wall span. Check the text and match, then confirm them.

i
Important: detection is not measurement.

This workflow ranks dimension-value boxes against dimension-line boxes, then proposes wall pairs whose facing edges bracket each line. These are axis-aligned geometric hypotheses, not verified measurements or CAD geometry. OCR suggestions are optional and unverified; every character, selected line and wall pair must be checked against the source drawing.

04 / MANUAL MEASUREMENT CHECK Check a dimension chain Manual values · independent arithmetic check

Enter values you have read from the drawing to check their arithmetic. If exactly one segment is blank, Planline can calculate a proposed remainder from the verified overall dimension and verified known segments. You must review and confirm it; it is not an OCR reading or a verified measurement.
