HOW THE WORKSPACES FIT TOGETHER

From scanned plan to reviewed output

  1. Import and inspect. Load a scan or PDF and review candidate regions.
  2. Verify the text. Use OCR suggestions as drafts; confirm every dimension manually.
  3. Trace and export. Draw geometry yourself and export only reviewed manual geometry.

IMPORTANT

Human review stays in control

Detector scores are not measurement confidence. OCR output is not verified automatically. Pixel scale is user-supplied, and automatic CAD reconstruction is not claimed.
