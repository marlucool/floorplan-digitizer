YOUR FIRST TASK

Three simple steps

  1. Upload your plan. Choose an image or PDF in the Drawing workspace.
  2. Check the suggestions. Review detected areas and confirm dimensions against the drawing.
  3. Draw and export. Trace the walls or rooms you need, then export your work.

IMPORTANT

Human review stays in control

Detector scores are not measurement confidence. OCR output is not verified automatically. Pixel scale is user-supplied, and automatic CAD reconstruction is not claimed.
